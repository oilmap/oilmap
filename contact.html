World Oil Map 2026

🌎🛢️

World Oil Map is a web-based Location Intelligence application supported and continuously updated using official country spatial database services and other authoritative sources related to the Oil & Gas sector.

This project is designed to support academic research, education, and the general public by providing interactive and dynamic access to global oil-related data.
