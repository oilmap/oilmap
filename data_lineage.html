Data Lineage and Sources

World Oil Map relies on official government, regulator and research datasets for oil and gas infrastructure. This page lists the source references compiled in the Oil and Gas Infrastructure Mapping (OGIM) database, version 3, with the country, region, infrastructure types and last visit date recorded for each source.
