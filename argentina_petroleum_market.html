Argentina's Major Petroleum Basins

Neuquén Basin & Vaca Muerta Shale
The undisputed epicenter of South American unconventional energy. Spanning Neuquén, Río Negro, La Pampa, and southern Mendoza, Vaca Muerta holds ~35° API light sweet crude (Medanito) and prolific dry/wet gas. Flagship concessions include Loma Campana, Bajada del Palo Oeste, La Amarga Chica, Fortín de Piedra, and Bandurria Sur.
Golfo San Jorge Basin (Chubut & Santa Cruz)
Argentina's pioneer mature oil basin (discovered 1907 in Comodoro Rivadavia). Produces Escalante Blend (~24° API), an internationally valued heavy sweet crude widely demanded by global coastal refiners for low-sulfur marine bunker fuel blending.
Austral & Tierra del Fuego Basin
Located in southern Patagonia and offshore southern waters. Major natural gas supplying region fed by offshore developments (Fénix, Carina-Aries) and the San Martín pipeline system to the central industrial corridor.
Midstream Expansion (Oldelval, Otasa, GPNK & Vaca Muerta Sur)
Massive pipeline buildouts including the Duplicar Project (Oldelval to Puerto Rosales), Otasa export line across the Andes to Chile's ENAP Talcahuano refinery, and the Gasoducto Perito Moreno (former GPNK) linking Tratayén to Salliqueló.

Medanito & Escalante Benchmark Dynamics

Medanito (~35° API, <0.2% sulfur) is Argentina's domestic light sweet crude generated from the Neuquén Basin. Its premium API yield drives modern cracking units. Escalante (~24.1° API, 0.19% sulfur) from Golfo San Jorge offers virtually zero sulfur with rich fuel oil fractions, making it an optimal blending component for IMO 2020 bunker specifications.
