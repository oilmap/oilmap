Cited by researchers, journalists and educators

World Oil Map is an independent, open-source location-intelligence project that maps oil and gas exploration, production and infrastructure using official national spatial data and other public sources. Below are independent publications, courses and recognitions that cite the project or use it as a data source. We list only mentions we have checked ourselves, and we describe each one as it appears at the source.

Der Tagesspiegel, interactive visual investigation · original German title: "Ölbohrung zwischen Giraffen" (English title translated by us)

How to cite World Oil Map

Please link to the canonical address https://oilmap.xyz/ and mention the date you accessed the data, since layers are updated over time.

Leal, R. (2026). World Oil Map [Interactive web map]. https://oilmap.xyz/
@misc{leal_worldoilmap,
  author = {Leal, Roque},
  title = {World Oil Map},
  year = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://oilmap.xyz/}}
}
