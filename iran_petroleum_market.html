Iran's Major Petroleum Basins & Infrastructure

Khuzestan Onshore Basin (Ahvaz, Marun, Gachsaran)
Accounting for the vast majority of Iran's oil production, southwestern Khuzestan province contains prolific giant onshore fields with high productivity per well and extensive gathering pipelines.
South Pars Gas Field (Persian Gulf)
Shared with Qatar (where it is known as the North Field), South Pars is the world's largest natural gas deposit, powering domestic consumption and petrochemical clusters.
Kharg Island Export Terminal
Located in the northern Persian Gulf, Kharg Island handles the vast majority of Iran's crude oil exports via deepwater berths and massive tank farms.
IGAT Pipeline Grid (Iranian Gas Trunkline)
An extensive high-pressure natural gas pipeline network spanning thousands of kilometers, connecting southern gas fields to northern consumption centers and export links.

Market Dynamics & Reserves

Iran holds the world's third-largest proven crude oil reserves (~208.6 billion barrels) and second-largest natural gas reserves. Despite international sanctions affecting investment and export channels, production capacity remains robust.
