Benchmarks explained

Brent
International light sweet benchmark from the North Sea, used as the primary reference for crude traded across Europe, Africa and the Middle East.
WTI
West Texas Intermediate, the U.S. light sweet benchmark (~39.6° API), priced at the Cushing, Oklahoma storage and pipeline hub.
Murban
Light sweet crude from Abu Dhabi (~39° API), a key physical and futures reference for Middle East exports to Asian refiners.
Merey
Venezuelan heavy sour crude blend (Merey 16), priced at a structural discount to light sweet benchmarks due to higher density and sulfur content.

How to read this dashboard

Change reflects the latest daily move reported in the price feed. Weekly and monthly ranges display the minimum and maximum prices for each period, while the gold dot marks where the current price sits within that corridor.

vs. Brent measures each blend's premium (+) or discount (–) relative to the Brent international benchmark, reflecting quality differentials (API gravity and sulfur) and freight logistics.
