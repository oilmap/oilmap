Brazil's Strategic Hydrocarbon Basins

Santos Basin Pre-Salt Cluster (Búzios, Tupi, Mero, Atapu, Sépia)
Located ~200-300 km offshore São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro beneath 2,000+ meters of water and 3,000+ meters of salt strata. The Santos Basin is the primary growth engine for Brazilian crude, tapped via ultra-deepwater FPSOs processing over 150-225 kbbl/d each.
Campos Basin (Marlim, Roncador, Jubarte, Albacora)
The historic cradle of Brazilian offshore deepwater exploration in Rio de Janeiro and Espírito Santo. Undergoing massive revitalization through subsea tiebacks, decommissioning of legacy units, and redevelopment by independent players like PRIO.
Margem Equatorial (Foz do Amazonas, Pará-Maranhão, Potiguar)
Brazil's northern frontier along the Guyana-Suriname conjugated margin. Represents significant exploration potential for light oil, subject to stringent environmental licensing.
Subsea Gas Trunklines (Rota 1, Rota 2, Rota 3)
Pipeline arteries transporting associated natural gas from deepwater Santos Basin to onshore gas processing facilities at Caraguatatuba, Cabiúnas, and the Gaslub Complex (Itaboraí).

Pre-Salt Quality & Market Dynamics

Lula (Tupi) and Búzios blends feature medium-to-light gravity (~28° to 30° API) and low sulfur content (<0.4%), making them premium feedstocks for complex refineries in Asia and Europe, consistently trading close to or at parity with Dated Brent.

Data Cleanliness & Filtering Logic

This dashboard strictly discards all vector records without verifiable identification. Every feature flagged as Unnamed, null, undefined, or blank in the upstream attributes is eliminated from the KPI calculations, top asset lists, and infrastructure distributions to ensure clean analytics.
