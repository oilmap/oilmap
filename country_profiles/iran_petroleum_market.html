Iran's Major Hydrocarbon Basins

Zagros Basin & Khuzestan Supergiants (Ahvaz, Marun, Gachsaran, Aghajari)
The Khuzestan plain in southwestern Iran hosts the majority of onshore oil reserves, with legendary carbonate reservoirs producing light to medium sour crudes connected via massive manifold systems toward Kharg Island.
South Pars Offshore Gas Dome (North Dome Extension)
Shared with Qatar in the Persian Gulf, South Pars is the world's largest gas reservoir. Developed in 24 operational phases by Petropars and POGC, it supplies both domestic gas demand and immense condensates blended for export.
Kharg Island & Persian Gulf Offshore Fields (IOOC)
Kharg Island handles approximately 90% of Iran's crude oil exports via deepwater loading berths, fed by offshore fields including Dorood, Foroozan, Aboozar, and Salman.
Goreh-Jask Crude Pipeline & Downstream Refining
The ~1,000 km Goreh-Jask pipeline allows crude routing around the Strait of Hormuz to the Gulf of Oman terminal. Downstream is anchored by the historic Abadan Refinery, Isfahan, Arak, and the Persian Gulf Star Condensate Refinery.

Iran Heavy Benchmark & Market Structure

Iran Heavy (~30.2° API, ~1.8% sulfur) is NIOC's premier export crude grade, priced in Asian markets with official selling prices (OSP) pegged against Oman/Dubai benchmarks. Domestic upstream activity is legally concentrated under the National Iranian Oil Company (NIOC) and specialized operational entities such as NISOC, IOOC, and Pars Oil and Gas Company (POGC).
