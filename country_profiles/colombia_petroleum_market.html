Colombia's Major Petroleum Basins

Llanos Orientales Basin (Cuenca Llanos)
Generating over 70% of Colombia's crude production (Rubiales, Quifa, Castilla, Chichimene, and Llanos 34 blocks). Produces heavy to medium crude (11° to 24° API), evacuated primarily via the Ocensa, ODC, and Bicentenario pipeline trunks to Caribbean export terminals.
Middle Magdalena Valley (Valle Medio del Magdalena - VMM)
Colombia's oldest historic basin (La Cira-Infantas discovered in 1918), characterized by medium crudes, mature waterflooding revitalization, and prospective unconventional Cretaceous La Luna shale plays surrounding Barrancabermeja.
Putumayo & Upper Magdalena (VSM) Basins
Southern fold belt fields delivering intermediate light-to-medium sweet crudes, transported across Andean topography via the OTA (Oleoducto Trasandino) to the Tumaco Pacific marine terminal.
Caribbean Offshore Gas Frontier (Guajira, Sinú & Tayrona)
Deepwater offshore provinces holding critical natural gas discoveries (Uchúva/Sirius, Gorgon, Kronos, Orca) pivotal for domestic gas self-sufficiency and transition power generation.

Vasconia & Castilla Benchmark Dynamics

Vasconia (~24.3° API, ~0.83% sulfur) and Castilla Blend (~18.8° API, ~1.97% sulfur) are Colombia's premier international crude export blends. Vasconia serves as an established regional benchmark for Gulf Coast and European complex refiners.
